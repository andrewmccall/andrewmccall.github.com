Hey! I'm Andrew. I build things with data and lead engineering teams.

I've been doing this for almost 20 years — from contributing to Apache HBase and Nutch in 2008 while running a location-based search startup, to leading data engineering at a ride-sharing startup, to currently serving as **Interim Executive Director** at **Lloyds Banking Group** (appointed twice).

## What I do

I work at the intersection of data engineering, platform engineering, and leadership:

- **Scalable distributed systems** — my first love, still going strong
- **Platform engineering** — Kubernetes, cloud-native, building internal platforms
- **Data engineering** — pipelines, quality, and making data actually useful
- **Engineering leadership** — building teams that ship

## This site

This is where I write about the technical stuff — deep dives into data systems, platform engineering, Kubernetes, and whatever I'm building. For the more professional angle (leadership, banking tech, industry thoughts), check my [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/drewmccall).
